## Open-source and research

### [ChromeRAG](https://github.com/pedapudibhargav/ChromeRAG)
[![PyPI](https://img.shields.io/pypi/v/chromerag.svg)](https://pypi.org/project/chromerag/)
[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/1378773845.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22970289)

HTML to RAG-ready Markdown. A 150 KB learned filter strips navigation, footers, cookie banners, related links and comments. On the held-out WCXB benchmark it scores F1 0.902 against Trafilatura's 0.860, CPU-only at about 36 ms per page.
`pip install chromerag` · [Results site](https://pedapudibhargav.github.io/ChromeRAG/) · paper submitted to SoftwareX

### [Vector Drift Study](https://github.com/pedapudibhargav/vector-drift-study)
A controlled study of how dense-retrieval Hit@k drifts as a corpus grows from 5k to 100k documents (frozen HNSW, two embedders, exact-search and BM25 controls). Code, data and a [per-query verification browser](https://pedapudibhargav.github.io/vector-drift-study/verify/) are public. IEEE Access manuscript in preparation.
